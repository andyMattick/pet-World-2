## 6.10 The Transatlantic Slave Trade: a hard lesson

- **A grown-up previews it first.** The skill has `gate:true, quiet:true` (see `HARD-LESSONS.md`). Its questions are first in `history6-review.md` under "Read first."
- **No celebrations.** Her answers save as usual, but there's no role card, no celebration and no arcade minutes.
- **How it's written.** The questions are factual with no graphic detail. Victims are named as people (Afonso I of Kongo, Domingos Álvares), and nothing weighs "both sides." The short answer asks for one cause and one effect.
- **Watch the video together.** Khan's video for this lesson, "Impact of the Slave Trade Through a Ghanaian Lens," is a good one for a grown-up to watch with her before she practices.

**Review.** All the questions, with the right answers in bold, are in `docs/history6-review.md`. They had a strict cold pass by the author, not a separate blind reviewer. Please skim them before she starts, and read 6.10 before you unlock it.
