# English Nouns and Verbs: changes for review

The correct answer is in **bold**. L1 is easier and L3 is harder. For each lesson:

- **New questions:** 4 harder ones (sentence fixes, find-the-error, words used as different parts of speech), for 14 in total.
- **Fixed choices:** built-in questions whose answer choices were rewritten because the right answer was the longest choice (or a wrong answer was confusing, like "cactuses only"). The prompt and the correct answer are unchanged.
- **Levels:** the difficulty tag given to each of the 10 built-in questions, in order.

Questions where students pick a word out of the sentence (like "Which word names a place? We ate lunch at the park.") were left alone even when the right word is longest.

Apply with `node docs/english-bank.mjs` from the repo root.
