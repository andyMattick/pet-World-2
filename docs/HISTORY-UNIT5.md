- **Videos.** Every Khan video in each lesson is its own ▶ button, 23 in all. Each lesson link opens the start of the Khan lesson. Watching counts as History time, and practice asks her to watch first (see `PRACTICE-TIME.md`).
- **Roles.** She plays a person from the time:
  - a Roman landowner in Gaul
  - Empress Theodora
  - an Irish monk
  - a French serf
  - a peasant marching with Wat Tyler
  - a Tang official
  - a Cairo merchant
  - a translator at the House of Wisdom
  - a Sogdian caravan merchant
  - Ibn Battuta
  - a Song merchant
- **Questions.** There are 157 multiple-choice questions, 14–15 per practice, at all three levels. Every practice has 2–4 short adapted sources: Salvian, Procopius, Einhard, a manor rule, John Ball, a Ming edict, Usama ibn Munqidh, al-Kindi, a Sogdian letter, Ibn Battuta and the Qingming scroll. There are also "which best explains why" questions and continuity-and-change questions. The wrong answers are written to be believable, no right answer is the longest choice, and `check-content` reports 0 warnings.
- **Newer-type questions.** Each practice has 2 scored newer-type questions (sort, select all, order, or Part A/B) and 1 short answer. The unit test has a paragraph.
- **Project.** 📁 Trade Web Travel Journal: follow one trade good or traveler across a web.
- **Test.** The unit test has 22 questions (2 per practice), and 17 right passes. The Khan unit test link is in `KHAN_UNIT_TESTS`.
- **Game.** ⚖️ Pax Oeconomica, the trade strategy game, with the Medieval empires (Byzantium, the Abbasids, Song, Mali, Srivijaya, Kievan Rus). See `PAX-OECONOMICA.md`.
