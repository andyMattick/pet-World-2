- **Videos.** Every Khan video in each lesson is its own ▶ button, 21 in all. Lesson 7.3 has all 10, including the two Haitian Revolution parts that Khan lists after the practice. Lesson 7.8 has none on Khan, so its 🎬 Watch first step opens the Khan lesson instead. Each lesson link opens the start of the Khan lesson.
- **Roles.** She plays someone from the time:
  - a printer in Paris
  - a free Black sailor carrying news of revolution
  - a volunteer in Garibaldi’s Red Shirts
  - an apprentice engineer in Manchester
  - Rifa’a al-Tahtawi
  - Ottilie Baader
  - Harriet Forten Purvis
  - Lin Zexu
  - Yaa Asantewaa

  None of the roles is an enslaver, a perpetrator or a colonizer.
- **Questions.** There are 135 multiple-choice questions, 15 per practice, at all three levels (about 40% at level 3). Every practice has short adapted sources:
  - Locke, Rousseau and Wollstonecraft
  - the two declarations
  - Mazzini and Bismarck
  - Tocqueville on Manchester
  - the Charter Oath
  - the 1832 child worker and the Communist Manifesto
  - Equiano
  - Lin Zexu’s letter and Kenyatta’s fable (retold)
  - the 1857 rebels

  The wrong answers are written to be believable, no right answer is the longest choice, and `check-content` reports 0 warnings.
- **Hard history.** Violence is stated plainly in one clause, with no graphic detail and no scored death tolls. Enslaved and colonized people act for their own freedom. The racist case for empire is described, not quoted, and one level-3 question asks why it was wrong. There are no gated (preview first) lessons in this unit.
- **Newer-type questions.** Each practice has 2 scored newer-type questions (sort, select all, put in order, or Part A/B) and 1 short answer. The unit test ends with a paragraph: what was the most powerful engine of change?
- **Projects (she picks one).**
  - 📰 Front Page: the front page of a newspaper from one year.
  - 🏭 Engines of Change Exhibit: follow one idea or machine from 1750 to today.
- **Test.** The unit test has 18 questions (2 per practice), and 14 right passes. The Khan unit test link is in `KHAN_UNIT_TESTS`.
- **Not added (yet).** There's no game (Andy will decide later) and no reward set (Units 1–5 have none).
