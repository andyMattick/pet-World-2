- **Videos.** Every Khan video in each lesson has its own ▶ button, 9 in all. Each lesson link opens the start of the Khan lesson. In 9.3 and 9.6, which have no videos, the 🎬 Watch-first prompt opens the Khan lesson instead (checked at 375px).
- **Contested topics (owner rule, Oct 28, 2026).** Genocides since 1945, 9/11, Standing Rock, climate change and the Anthropocene, and the inequality debate are introduced lightly, and she researches them.
  - **Scored questions in 9.6–9.8** cover only definitions, plain facts and dates, and reading what a short source says.
  - **Debates in 9.2–9.3** (is globalization good or bad, is the world flat or spiky) are shown as views people hold. No opinion is ever keyed.
  - **🔎 Research it** short answers (6 in all, in 9.3, 9.6, 9.7 and 9.8) send her to the Khan article or video with a grown-up. A grown-up reads them, and they are never scored.
  - **No gate or quiet flags** in this unit. Global Conflicts has no role card, so its feedback stays plain.
- **Roles.** She plays a person from the time:
  - a dockworker at the Port of Singapore (1999)
  - a reporter who covers the world economy (2009)
  - a retired Green Revolution plant scientist (2023)
  - a tour guide at UN headquarters
  - a museum curator
  - an ice-core scientist in Antarctica (2024)
- **Questions.** There are 101 multiple-choice questions (13–15 per practice, 39% at level 3).
  - **Sources:** the Zapatista declaration, Friedman and Florida, UDHR Article 1, the Genocide Convention, the 9/11 Commission Report, the 1951 Refugee Convention and Paul Crutzen.
  - **Fairness:** no right answer is the longest choice, no wrong answer uses a give-away word, and `check-content` reports 0 warnings.
- **Newer-type questions.** Each practice has 2 scored newer-type questions: 3 sort, 6 put in order, 2 two-part, 2 type the answer and 1 select all. Each practice also has 1–2 short answers, 11 in all. The unit test ends with a neutral paragraph about one global connection.
- **Projects (she picks one).**
  - 📁 Follow the Product (`history9`)
  - Then and Now Interview (`history9lab`)
  - Curate a Museum Exhibit (`history9lab2`)

  Each one has a 🔎 research or "describe both views fairly" step.
- **Test.** The unit test has 14 questions (2 per practice), and 11 right passes. The Khan unit test link is in `KHAN_UNIT_TESTS`.
