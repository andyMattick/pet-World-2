# Biology: 15 questions where the right answer was the longest choice

Only wrong answers were changed. The questions and right answers are the same, so question ids, saved progress and any teacher edits are not affected. A blind reviewer got all 15 right with the choices shuffled.

Right answers are in **bold**.

1. The stomach, intestines, and liver work together. What is a group of organs working together called?
   **An organ system** / A group of tissues / An organism / A population
2. A plant droops after days without water. Which cell part has lost the most water?
   **The central vacuole** / The cell nucleus / The cell membrane / The chloroplasts in its leaves
3. A visitor asks why animal cells can change shape but plant cells mostly cannot. What do you explain?
   **Animal cells lack a rigid cell wall** / Animal cells lack a nucleus inside / Plant cells lack a flexible cell membrane / Plant cells lack cytoplasm inside
4. Students counted bee visits in one hour. Flower A (bright, scented): 42 visits. Flower B (pale, no scent): 6 visits. Which claim is best supported?
   **Bright, scented flowers attract more bees** / Bees choose pale flowers whenever they can / Scent keeps most of the bees away / Bees visit flowers in a random order
5. A visitor asks how a hard seed coat helps a seed that has to survive winter. What do you say?
   **It can protect the embryo until conditions improve** / It lets the young seed grow quickly up through the snow / It stores water that the roots use in winter / It helps the seed attract animals to carry it
6. A visitor asks how new plants made by asexual reproduction compare to the parent. Offspring are:
   **Genetically identical to the parent** / A mix of traits from two different parents / A new species unlike the parent / Grown from pollen and egg cells
7. You show the group a stem cutting that grew roots and became a new plant. What is this an example of?
   **Asexual reproduction** / Sexual reproduction / Cross-pollination / Seed germination in the soil
8. Onions and tulips grow new bulbs underground from one parent bulb. What kind of reproduction is this?
   **Asexual reproduction** / Sexual reproduction / Cross-pollination / Seed dispersal by the wind
9. A visitor asks what is inside a seed. Which answer is best?
   **A tiny plant and stored food** / Pollen and sweet nectar for bees / A small flower and fruit / Water, air, and a root
10. A seed has a hard, waterproof coat and floats. Where would it most likely spread?
   **Along rivers and coasts** / On dry mountaintops / Stuck in the fur of passing animals / Right under its parent
11. Chewing is mechanical digestion. Which is an example of chemical digestion?
   **Saliva breaking down starch** / Teeth grinding food into small pieces / The tongue moving food / Swallowing a large bite
12. A kid notices a cracker starts to taste sweet after long chewing. What explains this?
   **Saliva breaks starch into sugar** / Chewing releases sugar hidden in the cracker / The tongue makes extra sugar / Air turns the starch to sugar
13. What does the pancreas send to the small intestine?
   **Digestive enzymes** / Saliva and mucus / Extra stomach acid / Red blood cells
14. An illness flattens the villi in the small intestine. What would most likely happen?
   **Fewer nutrients would be absorbed** / More water would be absorbed / Food would be chewed more slowly / The stomach would make much less acid
15. Which systems deliver the oxygen that digestive organs need?
   **Respiratory and circulatory** / Skeletal and muscular / Nervous and skeletal / Digestive and immune systems
